Example Lending Ltd
Fairness and explainability audit: personal loan scoring model, version 3.2
Fictional sample for a software demonstration. No real people or data.

1. Scope
The model scores personal loan applications. This report covers approval outcomes for the 2026 review period.

2. Fairness results
Approval rates were compared across applicant groups. The approval rate for Group B was 0.74 of the rate for Group A, which the team recorded against the 80% rule.
A second check applied the four-fifths
rule to the same groups and reached the same conclusion.
Reviewers said four-fifths of reviewers agreed that the outcome was acceptable.

The status panel stays lime green while the check passes.

3. Explainability
Individual decisions were explained with SHAP, which attributes each score to the input features.

The explanation step runs as follows:
    import shap
    explainer.shap_values(applicants)

A cross-check was run with LIME on 200 declined applications, using local interpretable model-agnostic explanations.

4. Limitations
The explanation methods are post hoc. Their outputs were not checked against independent ground truth in this review.
Sampling covered one review period only.
